About Dr. (Mrs) Iniobong Abiola-Awe

Iniobong Abiola-Awe was born and raised in Nigeria. From an early age she showed interest in environmental sustainability and community development, which later shaped her academic and professional journey.


Education

She completed her first and second degrees studies in Biological Sciences Zoology and Parasitology with additional Professional Masters degree in Environmental Management. She earned a Ph.D in Environmental Management with focus in Climate Change Policy.


Career

Her academic work includes research on adaptation strategies and resilience planning. With a career of over 25 years, she has worked across government and international agencies to design and implement climate-related programs. She currently serves as the Director and Head of the Department of Climate Change at the Federal Ministry of Environment.


Research & Interests

Her primary research interests include climate adaptation, community resilience, and mainstreaming climate action into sector policy. She has authored several policy briefs and papers used by practitioners and policymakers.
